# A linter turns taste into a count

A linter turns taste into a count, and a count is something a team can
argue with.

A style rule that lives in one reviewer's head fails whenever that reviewer
is away. Written as a check, the same rule runs on every commit and names
the line. The argument moves from "I would not have written it that way" to
"the rule fired here, and this is why it exists".

The rest needs a reader. A regex decides shape: a heading present, a
sentence over a length, a banned word. Whether a paragraph says what its
heading claims is a judgement, so it goes to a rubric and a second model.
That model quotes its evidence in at most 200 characters, the cap that
PROMPT-001's verdict schema sets.
