## Your Monday starts with the exceptions

Every matched line is already done when you sit down. You open a list of
the lines Reconcile could not match, each with the closest candidate and
the amount that differs. You work the list, not the ledger.

## You see why before you accept a match

Reconcile matches on amount, date, and reference, and each match shows
which of the three it used. When one is off, you see why before you accept
it, and no match counts until you do.

## One bank, one ledger, one afternoon

Connect the bank feed and the ledger, then let the first run match the
backlog. Review the exceptions the next morning.

## Questions finance teams ask

**Does Reconcile change our ledger?** No. It reads the ledger and the bank
feed and writes nothing back. You post the corrections yourself.

**What if our bank has no feed?** Upload the statement as CSV. Reconcile
treats it the same as a feed.

**What happens after the 14 days?** The account pauses. Your matches stay
readable, and a card turns it back on.
