# Long Norrland Pulp (NPUL): a quality producer at six times trough earnings

## Idea

Buy Norrland Pulp, the Stockholm-listed softwood kraft pulp producer, at
SEK 84. We expect the shares to reach about SEK 96 within twelve months as
the market gives the company credit for its cost position.

## Consensus

The street rates the shares Hold or Sell: nine of eleven analysts, per the
company's own analyst page dated 14 August 2026. Consensus 2027 EBITDA is
SEK 2.1 billion, on the view that Kaarna Fibre's Kaarna II mill adds 600
thousand tonnes of softwood pulp to the Nordic market in 2027 and pushes
NBSK prices below USD 1,000 a tonne. Two initiations in July, from Hägglund
Securities and Brenner & Co, name Kaarna II as the reason for their Sell
ratings.

## Variant perception

We agree that Kaarna II adds supply in 2027 and that prices soften. Our
view is that Norrland Pulp handles the downturn better than the street
expects, because of its cost position, so we expect 2027 EBITDA to come in
a little above consensus, perhaps SEK 2.3 billion rather than SEK 2.1
billion.

## Why we hold this view

Norrland Pulp sits in the second quartile of the Nordic softwood cost
curve, per slide 12 of the company's capital markets day deck from May
2026, and its wood supply is contracted at fixed prices through 2027, per
page 9 of the Q2 2026 interim report. Second-quartile cash costs
stayed below the trough NBSK price in both 2019 and 2023, per the Nordic
Pulp Association's 2025 cost survey.

## Trigger

There is no single event. We expect the market to recognise the quality
of the asset base over time and the shares to re-rate toward their peers.

## Payoff

At SEK 84 the shares trade at six times consensus 2027 EBITDA: SEK 8.4
billion of market value and SEK 4.2 billion of net debt at 30 June 2026,
per the Q2 report. On our SEK 2.3 billion at the same multiple, with net
debt unchanged, the shares are worth about SEK 96, some 14 percent above
today.

## Bear case

A consensus holder would say: a second-quartile cost position does not
matter when the marginal tonne sets the price, and Kaarna II moves the
marginal tonne. We answer: Norrland Pulp's fixed wood contracts hold its
cash cost flat through 2027 while peers face spot wood prices, per the
same Q2 report, so we expect its margin over the marginal producer to
widen in a downturn rather than narrow.

## What kills it

If Norrland Pulp reopens its 2027 wood contracts, or reports a cash cost
above the Nordic median in any quarter, the cost-position view is wrong
and we sell the position. If pulp prices fall below USD 950 a tonne for a
full quarter, we halve the position and review.
