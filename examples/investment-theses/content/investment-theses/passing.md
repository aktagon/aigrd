# Long Norrland Pulp (NPUL): the 2027 supply glut the street prices in is not coming

## Idea

Buy Norrland Pulp, the Stockholm-listed softwood kraft pulp producer, at
SEK 84. We expect the shares to reach about SEK 130 within twelve months as
2027 pulp supply estimates come down.

## Consensus

The street rates the shares Hold or Sell: nine of eleven analysts, per the
company's own analyst page dated 14 August 2026. Consensus 2027 EBITDA is
SEK 2.1 billion, on the view that Kaarna Fibre's Kaarna II mill adds 600
thousand tonnes of softwood pulp to the Nordic market in 2027 and pushes
NBSK prices below USD 1,000 a tonne. Two initiations in July, from Hägglund
Securities and Brenner & Co, name Kaarna II as the reason for their Sell
ratings.

## Variant perception

In our view Kaarna II does not ship before 2029. The consensus counts it as
2027 supply; we count it as none. Without it we expect the Nordic softwood
market to be short about 400 thousand tonnes in 2027, prices to hold near
today's USD 1,180, and 2027 EBITDA to land nearer SEK 2.9 billion than
SEK 2.1 billion.

## Why we know this and the street does not

Three facts, each in the public record and none in a sell-side model we
have seen:

- Kaarna Fibre's Q2 2026 interim report (page 7) moved "first pulp" from
  the second half of 2027 to "2029 at the earliest", after the environmental
  permit was appealed.
- The appeal, filed with the regional environmental court on 3 June 2026
  and listed in the court's public register, has a hearing in November
  2026. The court's last four mill appeals took twelve to eighteen months
  from hearing to ruling, per the same register.
- Kaarna Fibre's board has not approved the SEK 9 billion financing. The
  Q2 report describes it as "under negotiation".

The two Sell initiations were written before the Q2 report. The Hold notes
since then repeat the 2027 date without citing a source for it.

## Trigger

Kaarna Fibre reports Q3 on 24 October 2026. Management must either confirm
the 2029 date, which forces the street to move its supply model, or attach
approved financing to a 2027 date, which ends our thesis. The court's
ruling on the appeal, which we expect in the first half of 2027, is the
second trigger.

## Payoff

At SEK 84 the shares trade at six times consensus 2027 EBITDA: SEK 8.4
billion of market value and SEK 4.2 billion of net debt at 30 June 2026,
per the Q2 report. On our SEK 2.9 billion at the same multiple, with net
debt unchanged, the shares are worth about SEK 130, some 55 percent above
today. We expect the first step on 24 October and the rest as 2027
estimates move.

## Bear case

A consensus holder would say: even without Kaarna II, Brazilian hardwood
pulp lands in Rotterdam at a USD 300 discount and caps softwood pricing, so
the 2027 supply detail does not matter. We answer: Norrland Pulp sells into
liquid packaging board, whose long-fibre share is set by the board grade,
and in 2023 the hardwood discount widened to USD 340 while European
softwood volumes did not move, per the Nordic Pulp Association's monthly
statistics.

## What kills it

If the Q3 report keeps a 2027 start with approved financing, the variant
perception is wrong and we sell the position that day. If the court
dismisses the appeal before the Q3 report, we halve the position and
review after the report.
